# SECTION 1: MEXICAN NATIONAL HOLIDAYS AND PUENTES, 2027

## 1.1 The legal spine

Mexico has **seven** días de descanso obligatorio under **Article 74 of the Ley Federal del Trabajo**. Four are fixed dates. Three were moved to Mondays by the 2006 reform specifically to create long weekends and stimulate domestic tourism. Working one of these obliges the employer to pay triple wages under Art. 75 LFT.

Sources: [El Universal, Días Festivos en México 2027](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/fechas/festivos-mexico-2027/) and [Calendario de México, Días Festivos Oficiales 2027 (Art. 74 LFT)](https://calendariodemexico.com/dias-festivos-oficiales-mexico-2027/). Both sources agree on every date below.

| Holiday | 2027 date | Day | Puente? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Año Nuevo | 1 January | **Friday** | Yes, Fri to Sun |
| Día de la Constitución | **1 February** (moved) | **Monday** | Yes, Sat 30 Jan to Mon 1 Feb |
| Natalicio de Benito Juárez | **15 March** (moved) | **Monday** | Yes, Sat 13 to Mon 15 Mar |
| Día del Trabajo | 1 May | **Saturday** | **No. Falls on a Saturday.** |
| Independencia | 16 September | **Thursday** | Partial. Thu to Sun if Friday is given. |
| Aniversario de la Revolución | **15 November** (moved) | **Monday** | Yes, Sat 13 to Mon 15 Nov |
| Navidad | 25 December | **Saturday** | **No standalone puente.** Absorbed into the school break. |

**Critical 2027 quirk, and the whole year turns on it:** both **1 May and 25 December fall on Saturdays**, and **1 January 2028 also falls on a Saturday**. Two of your four fixed holidays generate **no extra day off**. Meanwhile all three moveable Mondays land cleanly. So in 2027 the money is in **February, March, November and December**, not May.

**Second critical quirk:** **Día de las Madres, 10 May 2027, falls on a Monday.** Mother's Day in Mexico is always 10 May regardless of weekday, so in most years it is a single restaurant-lunch event. In 2027 it sits on a Monday, which makes a **Saturday-to-Monday Mother's Day weekend commercially real for the first time in years**. This is the single most under-priced opportunity on this calendar for a domestic-focused property.

## 1.2 The school calendar is the real domestic demand driver

The SEP published the official 2026-2027 basic education calendar. These dates are confirmed by the Subsecretaría de Educación Básica.
Source: [SEP, Publica SEP Calendario Escolar 2026-2027](https://educacionbasica.sep.gob.mx/publica-sep-calendario-escolar-2026-2027-para-educacion-basica-y-normal/)

| Period | Dates | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Winter break | **21 Dec 2026 to 5 Jan 2027**, classes resume **7 Jan** | Covers the entire Zamna window |
| **Semana Santa break** | **22 Mar to 3 Apr 2027**, classes resume **5 Apr** | **Two full weeks, not one** |
| School year ends | **9 July 2027** (basic), 13 July (normal) | Summer season starts **Sat 10 July** |
| Teacher suspension days in 2027 | 1 Jan, 6 Jan, 1 Feb, 15 Mar, 5 May | 6 Jan and 5 May are school-only, not LFT holidays |

**Two operational consequences:**
- The Semana Santa domestic window is **13 nights (22 March to 3 April)**, not the 4 nights of Jueves Santo to Pascua. Price and package the full fortnight.
- **6 January (Reyes)** and **5 May** are school holidays but not legal work holidays. Parents with school-age kids are free, the office is not. This favours **short mid-week domestic escapes** and **family day-pass revenue**, not 3-night stays.

### Año Nuevo: Friday 1 January 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED (Art. 74 LFT).**
**Who travels:** In Tulum, New Year is majority **foreign**, high-spend, 25 to 45, plus affluent CDMX and Monterrey. It is the highest ADR night of the entire year in Tulum.
**Booking lead time:** 4 to 8 months. Foreign NYE buyers commit in July to September. Do not leave NYE inventory open past October.
**The offer: "Cuarenta y Seis" full-property New Year buyout.**
Sell the entire 46-villa property as a single closed-community New Year residency, 28 December to 3 January, minimum 6 nights, one price. Cidro becomes a private restaurant for the group. Pitch it to two buyer types: (a) an event promoter needing artist and VIP housing during the Zamna window, (b) a multi-family Mexican group (three to five families, 40 to 100 people) who want a gated compound rather than a hotel. A gated 46-villa property is one of the only assets in Tulum that can say yes to a 100-person private New Year. Nobody else in La Veleta can.
**Fallback if no buyout:** minimum 5 nights, no single-night NYE sales, and a mandatory **Cena de Año Nuevo at Cidro** priced per person and charged to the villa. That mandatory F&B charge is where the margin is.

### Día de Reyes and the Guadalupe-Reyes tail: 12 December to 6 January
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** Día de Reyes 2027 is **Wednesday 6 January**. Guadalupe-Reyes runs from **Día de la Virgen de Guadalupe, Sunday 12 December 2027**, to **Reyes, 6 January 2028**. Both dates confirmed by [El Universal 2027 calendar](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/fechas/festivos-mexico-2027/).
**Who travels:** Purely domestic. Guadalupe-Reyes is a 26-day cultural marathon that has no foreign equivalent. Foreigners in Tulum during this period are there for the festivals and do not know what Guadalupe-Reyes is.
**Booking lead time:** 6 to 10 weeks for the December leg, 3 to 6 weeks for the January tail.
**The offer: "Rosca de Reyes en Cidro" plus the Guadalupe-Reyes Residency.**
Two products.
1. **6 January day product:** a Rosca de Reyes afternoon at Cidro, open to non-guests, priced per head, with the traditional muñeco rule and a tamale-night IOU for whoever finds it. That IOU is redeemable **2 February, Día de la Candelaria**, which gives you a booked reason to bring the same domestic customer back four weeks later in your weakest early-February midweek.
2. **Guadalupe-Reyes Residency:** a 26-night long-stay rate for remote workers, sold on the co-working space, from 12 December to 6 January. Foreign digital nomads take this and it fills the villas that the festival crowd will not book because they are not beachfront.

### Día de la Constitución puente: Saturday 30 January to Monday 1 February 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** Constitution Day is legally observed on the **first Monday of February**, which is **1 February 2027**. The historical date, 5 February, falls on a Friday and is not the day off.
**Who travels:** Overwhelmingly **domestic**. This is the first long weekend of the Mexican year and the release valve after the January financial hangover (the "cuesta de enero"). Sectur consistently ranks Cancún and Riviera Maya in the national top three for puente arrivals. ([La Jornada / Sectur](https://www.jornada.com.mx/2026/03/13/economia/020n2eco))
**Booking lead time:** 3 to 5 weeks. This is the classic 28-day Mexican booking window. Your ad spend should start **26 December**, not February.
**The offer: "Primer Puente, Tres Noches."**
A flat-price 3-night domestic package, Friday to Monday, in MXN, with **MSI (meses sin intereses) at checkout**. This is the single highest-leverage thing an owner can do for the Mexican market and most Tulum properties do not offer it. Mexican consumers plan holidays around 3, 6 and 9 months interest-free credit. Include one dinner at Cidro and unlimited sauna and cold plunge. Sell it in Spanish only. Target CDMX, Monterrey, Guadalajara and Querétaro, all of which have domestic air service into Tulum's Felipe Carrillo Puerto airport (TQO). ([Tulum International Airport, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulum_International_Airport))

### Natalicio de Benito Juárez puente: Saturday 13 to Monday 15 March 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** Observed on the **third Monday of March**, which is **15 March 2027**. Juárez's actual birthday, 21 March, falls on the Sunday of the following weekend and is **not** the day off.
**Who travels:** Domestic, heavily families. March is warm, pre-sargassum-peak, and school is still in session, so this puente is the last clean 3-night family window before the two-week Easter break.
**Booking lead time:** 3 to 5 weeks.
**The offer: "Puente de Marzo, Niños Gratis."**
Kids under 12 stay free and eat free at Cidro, two children per villa. The villa format is your advantage over a hotel room here: a family of five fits in one unit without an extra-bed conversation. Bundle in a cenote trip and the pool. The economics work because you are selling a villa that would otherwise be empty and recovering the child cost on parents' dinner and bar spend.
**Strategic note:** 15 March is **one week before** the SEP Semana Santa break opens on 22 March. A well-designed offer stitches them: sell a "stay 13 March, return 22 March at the same rate" voucher to convert one puente guest into two stays.

### Semana Santa and Pascua 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.**
- **Jueves Santo: Thursday 25 March 2027**
- **Viernes Santo: Friday 26 March 2027**
- **Sábado de Gloria: Saturday 27 March 2027**
- **Domingo de Pascua: Sunday 28 March 2027**
- **SEP school break: Monday 22 March to Saturday 3 April 2027** (13 days)

Sources: [El Universal 2027 calendar](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/fechas/festivos-mexico-2027/), [Calendario de México, Semana Santa 2027](https://calendariodemexico.com/semana-santa-2027/), [SEP school calendar](https://educacionbasica.sep.gob.mx/publica-sep-calendario-escolar-2026-2027-para-educacion-basica-y-normal/).

**Important legal caveat, state it in your pricing meetings:** Semana Santa is **not** a legal holiday under Art. 74 LFT. Employers grant it by contract or custom, not by law. That means **office-worker parents are not automatically free for 13 days even though their children are.** The real domestic shape is: schools out for two weeks, adults take **one** of those two weeks, and the split skews to the second week (Jueves Santo onward).

**Who travels:** This is **the** domestic Mexican travel week of the year, comparable to nothing else. Also significant foreign traffic, but the domestic wave is bigger and more price-elastic.
**Booking lead time:** 6 to 12 weeks for domestic, longer for the beachfront comp set. Have your Semana Santa rate live by **early January**.
**The offer: "Quincena Santa" plus the "Pascua Villa Takeover."**
1. **Quincena Santa:** a 13-night rate for the full school break at a heavy per-night discount, aimed at families with one remote-working parent. The co-working space is the closing argument: mum or dad works, kids and the other parent are at the pool. This is the only offer that monetises the **first** week (22 to 26 March), which the whole town under-sells because it chases the Easter weekend.
2. **Pascua Villa Takeover:** blocks of 4 to 8 adjoining villas for extended families, 25 to 29 March, with a private **Comida de Pascua at Cidro on Sunday 28 March** (whole grilled fish, no meat on Viernes Santo on the 26th, that menu distinction matters to Mexican Catholic guests and signals you understand them).

### Día del Niño and Día del Trabajo: Friday 30 April and Saturday 1 May 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** Día del Niño, 30 April, is Friday. Día del Trabajo, 1 May, is Saturday and is an Art. 74 obligatory rest day.
**Honest assessment:** **1 May 2027 is a dud as a puente** because it falls on a Saturday and generates no additional day off. Do not build a campaign around "the May long weekend" in 2027. There isn't one.
**What is actually there:** Friday 30 April (Día del Niño) into Sunday 2 May is a normal weekend with a strong emotional hook for families with small children, and sargassum is usually still moderate in late April.
**Who travels:** Regional domestic, drive-market and short-haul. Mérida, Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Chetumal, Campeche. Not a fly-in weekend.
**Booking lead time:** 2 to 3 weeks. Very short. Regional Mexican drive traffic books almost last-minute.
**The offer: "Día del Niño Pool Day" as a non-resident day product.**
Since you will not fill 46 villas on this weekend, monetise the asset base instead. Sell a **Friday 30 April day pass** to local and regional families: pool, kids' activity, and a kids-eat-free Cidro lunch, priced per family. Simultaneously sell a **counter-programmed adult product**: "Sin Niños," Saturday 1 May, sauna, cold plunge, yoga, long lunch, adults only, for the local Tulum and Playa professional market. Two products, same weekend, opposite audiences, on an asset that is otherwise idle.

### Día de las Madres: **Monday 10 May 2027**
**Confidence: CONFIRMED** as a date (10 May is fixed in Mexico regardless of weekday). Not an Art. 74 holiday. ([El Universal 2027 calendar](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/fechas/festivos-mexico-2027/))
**Why this matters more than its ranking suggests:** Mother's Day in Mexico is a bigger consumer-spend event than Mother's Day in the US, and it is culturally obligatory. Because 10 May 2027 lands on a **Monday**, Saturday 8 to Monday 10 May reads as a genuine three-day weekend to a Mexican family, even though nobody is legally off. Many employers and most schools go soft that Monday.
**Who travels:** Domestic, multi-generational. Adult children bring their mother. Average party size is larger and older than any other window on this calendar. They want comfort, shade, good food and no techno.
**Booking lead time:** 4 to 6 weeks. Slightly longer than a standard puente because the group has to coordinate three generations.
**The offer: "Fin de Semana de Mamá," 8 to 10 May, three nights.**
Structured for the multi-generational group: a villa for the parents plus an adjoining villa for the adult children, sold as a pair at a package price. Inclusions that fit the actual guest, not a generic spa cliché: a **gentle restorative yoga session** (not power vinyasa), **sauna without the cold plunge as the default** (offer the plunge, do not centre it, this audience is 55+), a long **Comida de Día de las Madres at Cidro on Monday 10 May** with the table held all afternoon, and a printed family photo. Sell it starting **late March**, in Spanish, targeted at adult children aged 30 to 50 in CDMX and Monterrey, not at the mothers.
**Also sell it to the town:** open the 10 May Cidro comida to Tulum residents as a paid ticket. Tulum's local professional and business-owner population is growing and has nowhere good to take their mothers.

### Independencia: Wednesday 15 to Thursday 16 September 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** 16 September is an Art. 74 fixed holiday and falls on a **Thursday** in 2027. El Grito is the night of **Wednesday 15 September**.
**The puente question:** Thursday 16 to Sunday 19 September becomes a four-day weekend **only if the employer grants Friday 17**. Many do. Treat it as a probable but not guaranteed 4-day window and price flexibly: 2-night minimum, not 4.
**Who travels:** Almost entirely **domestic**. Foreigners avoid September (hurricane season, low season). This is your one meaningful domestic demand spike inside your worst month, and it is worth a disproportionate amount of effort.
**Booking lead time:** 2 to 4 weeks.
**The offer: "Noche Mexicana en Cidro," 15 September, and the "Puente Patrio" 4-night rate.**
The Grito night event is the real money-maker here because it sells to **non-guests**: Tulum's domestic residents, Playa del Carmen and Akumal locals, and the domestic tourists staying elsewhere. Run a ticketed Noche Mexicana at Cidro on Wednesday 15 September: pozole, chiles en nogada (still in season into September), mezcal, a live Grito at 11pm, mariachi. Charge per head. Cap it. Sell out.
Attach a **"Puente Patrio"** villa rate, Wednesday 15 to Sunday 19, with the Grito dinner included and a **free-cancellation-until-7-days** policy to catch the households still waiting to hear whether they get Friday off. Free cancellation is what converts this specific window.
**Honest note:** September is peak hurricane season in the Mexican Caribbean. A flexible cancellation policy is not generosity here, it is the price of selling September at all.

### Día de Muertos and Hanal Pixán: Saturday 30 October to Tuesday 2 November 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED** for the dates. **1 November falls on a Monday and 2 November on a Tuesday in 2027**, creating a natural Saturday-to-Tuesday four-day run. Neither day is an Art. 74 legal holiday, but 2 November is widely taken and appears as a school suspension day in SEP calendars.
**Regional variant:** In the Yucatán Peninsula the tradition is **Hanal Pixán**, "food of the souls" in Maya, observed 31 October (children), 1 November (adults), 2 November (all souls), with *pib* cooked in an earth oven. ([Plalla, Day of the Dead in Tulum](https://plalla.com/en/day-of-the-dead-in-tulum-events-traditions/)) This is **not** the Mexico City parade version, and saying so is a genuine differentiator.
**Who travels:** **Both audiences, hard.** Domestic Mexicans travelling for the holiday, plus a large and growing foreign contingent for whom Day of the Dead is now a bucket-list travel event. This is the only date on the calendar where both of your markets peak simultaneously.
**Booking lead time:** 8 to 16 weeks. Foreign Day of the Dead buyers book in July and August.
**The offer: "Hanal Pixán: La Mesa de los Ausentes."**
A four-night package, 30 October to 3 November, built around one genuinely Yucatecan thing rather than a generic marigold photo wall.
- A **community altar in the AMARI courtyard** built over the four days. Guests are invited before arrival to send a photograph and the name of someone they have lost. The altar is assembled with those photographs. This costs almost nothing and produces the emotional and social-media payload of a far more expensive event.
- A **pib dinner at Cidro on 1 November**, cooked in a real pib (earth oven) dug in the grounds, with a Maya cook from the local community paid properly and credited by name. Open to non-guests as a ticketed dinner.
- **Do not** sell it as "Day of the Dead party." Sell it as "the Yucatán version, which almost no visitor has seen."
**Also:** the **Festival de Tradiciones de Vida y Muerte at Xcaret** runs the same window, 30 October to 2 November, and pulls heavy domestic traffic into the Riviera Maya. ([Riviera Maya official](https://rivieramaya.mx/en/life-and-death-festival-xcaret/), [Festival de Vida y Muerte official](https://www.festivaldevidaymuerte.com/en/)) Confirm 2027 dates when published. Offer a **Xcaret shuttle plus 3pm late checkout** add-on; the festival runs late (roughly 3:45pm to 10:30pm) and guests come back exhausted.

### Revolución puente: Saturday 13 to Monday 15 November 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED.** Observed on the **third Monday of November**, which is **15 November 2027**. The historical date, 20 November, falls on a Saturday in 2027 and is not the day off.
**Who travels:** Domestic. Mid-November is one of the best weather windows of the entire year in Tulum: sargassum season is over, humidity has dropped, hurricane season is closing, and international rates have not yet spiked.
**Booking lead time:** 3 to 5 weeks.
**The offer: "Puente Fit," a 3-night performance reset.**
November is when the Mexican professional class starts thinking about the December damage. Sell the puente as a contrarian product: three mornings of training in the gym, daily sauna and cold plunge protocol, yoga, a protein-forward Cidro menu, and zero alcohol as the default with alcohol available on request. Target men and women 30 to 45 in CDMX and Monterrey, the same demographic that buys Hyrox and F45. This is the offer your gym, sauna and cold plunge were built for and almost nobody in Tulum is selling it to Mexicans in Spanish.
**Adjacent demand:** **IRONMAN Cozumel** and **GFNY Cozumel** both run in November. In 2026 they were 22 November and 8 November respectively. ([This Is Cozumel, IRONMAN](https://thisiscozumel.com/tourist-info/100-cozumel-events-and-public-holidays/731-ironman-cozumel), [Isla Cozumel Resorts events calendar](https://islacozumelresorts.com/cozumel-events-calendar/)) 2027 dates were not published at time of research, **TYPICAL: late November for IRONMAN, early November for GFNY**. Verify with the organisers before selling. See the Taper Camp offer in Section 2.

**Confidence: CONFIRMED** for the legal dates. **Nochebuena is Friday 24 December 2027, Navidad is Saturday 25 December 2027.** Dates from [El Universal 2027 calendar](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/fechas/festivos-mexico-2027/).

The pricing error most Tulum owners make is running one flat December rate; the 22 to 27 December guest and the 28 December to 3 January guest are different people paying different money.

# SECTION 2: TULUM AND QUINTANA ROO SPECIFIC EVENTS

## 2.1 The winter electronic music season: your ADR engine

Tulum's December-to-February electronic music season is the highest-rate period of the year, and **La Veleta / Uptown is where the people who cannot afford or cannot get beachfront actually sleep.** A gated 46-villa property in La Veleta is structurally well positioned for this season: the beach road is congested and expensive, and festival venues are inland anyway.

### Zamna Tulum, 10th anniversary season

## 2.3 The equinoxes

### Spring equinox: Saturday 20 to Sunday 21 March 2027
**Confidence: CONFIRMED for the astronomical date.** The 2027 spring equinox falls **20 March**, and the Kukulcán descent at Chichén Itzá is observed on **21 March**, which in 2027 is a **Sunday**. One source reports free entry at Chichén Itzá on that day; verify with INAH before promising it. ([Vive Cancún, equinox guide 2027](https://vivecancun.com/blog/equinoccio-de-primavera/))
The shadow phenomenon is visible roughly **3:00pm to 4:30pm** and is observable with less definition on the days either side.
**Tulum ruins also have equinox alignments.** At the **Templo del Dios Descendente**, a ray of sunlight aligns under the descending-god relief at the spring equinox, and El Castillo at Tulum is oriented to the equinox sunrise over the Caribbean. ([Tulum Times, El Castillo at Tulum](https://tulumtimes.com/significance-of-el-castillo-at-tulum-ruins), [Investment Properties Mexico, Tulum ruins equinox](https://blog.investmentpropertiesmexico.com/lifestyle/beachfront-tulum-ruins-get-1000s-of-spring-equinox-visitors)) Tulum ruins draw thousands of equinox visitors and are **fifteen minutes from La Veleta**, whereas Chichén Itzá is a 2.5 to 3 hour drive.
**Who travels:** A genuine blend. Domestic Mexicans (including a large spiritual and new-age domestic segment from CDMX and Guadalajara) plus foreign wellness travellers.
**Booking lead time:** 6 to 10 weeks.
**The convergence that makes 2027 special:** the equinox weekend (20 to 21 March) sits **immediately before** the SEP Semana Santa break opening on Monday 22 March, and **one week after** the Benito Juárez puente. **Friday 12 March to Sunday 4 April 2027 is one continuous 24-day sellable domestic block.** Nobody should be quoting nightly rates in that window.
**The offer: "Equinoccio 4:44."**
A 3-night equinox retreat, 19 to 22 March, using your yoga studio and the fact that you are minutes from the Tulum ruins rather than three hours from Chichén Itzá. Sunrise practice on the equinox morning, a guided pre-opening visit to the Tulum archaeological zone, a temazcal or sound bath, plant-forward Cidro menus, sauna and cold plunge circuit. Price it above a normal March weekend and sell it in **both Spanish and English**, because this is one of the rare products where both audiences want the same thing.
**Do not** claim a specific light phenomenon will be visible on a specific minute at Tulum ruins. Describe the alignment, not a guaranteed spectacle.

### Autumn equinox: Wednesday 22 to Thursday 23 September 2027
**Confidence: TYPICAL** (astronomical date around 22 to 23 September; confirm the exact date and the INAH observance day closer to time). The Kukulcán descent repeats at the autumn equinox but draws far smaller crowds than spring.
**Why it matters here:** it falls inside **September, your worst month**, and immediately after the Independencia puente. It is one of only two demand hooks in your weakest window.
**The offer: "Reset de Otoño," a 5-night low-season wellness residency** at an aggressive rate, aimed at domestic remote workers. Yoga twice daily, sauna and cold plunge protocol, co-working desk included, Cidro half-board. Sell the low season as the feature: *"the equinox, without the crowd."*

## 2.4 Natural seasons

### Whale shark season
**Dates: SEASONAL. Officially enforced roughly 15 May to 17 September**, with most operators running **1 June to 15 September**. Peak is **July to August**, when the La Afuera aggregation east of Holbox can hold 200 to 400 animals and is the largest known aggregation in the world. CONANP permits are required and tours outside the season are illegal. ([Holbox Whale Shark Tours](https://holboxwhalesharktours.com/the-season-and-the-migration/), [Mr. Playas, Holbox whale shark season](https://mrplayas.com/holbox/whale-shark-season))
**Who travels:** Mixed. Strong domestic family appeal in the July-August school holidays, plus foreign eco-tourists.
**Booking lead time:** 2 to 6 weeks.
**Honest constraint:** the tours depart from **Isla Mujeres, Cancún or Holbox**, not Tulum. From La Veleta this is a very long day: pre-dawn departure, 2.5 to 3.5 hours each way.
**The offer: "Tiburón Ballena Early Rise."**
Do not sell the tour. Sell the thing that makes the tour survivable. A 4am private transfer with the vehicle stocked, a **Cidro breakfast box packed the night before**, a guaranteed 3pm-return late lunch held for the guest, and **sauna and cold plunge reserved for 5pm** on return day. Bundle it into a July or August family stay, which is exactly the SEP summer-holiday window (from 9 July 2027).

### Sea turtle nesting and hatching
**Dates: SEASONAL. Nesting May to October, peaking June to September. Hatching and releases July to November, peaking October.** ([Loco Gringo, turtle nesting season Riviera Maya](https://www.locogringo.com/blog/activities/when-sea-turtle-nesting-season-riviera-maya), [Hotel Cabañas Tulum, turtle season](https://www.hotelcabanastulum.com/blog/sea-turtle-nesting-season-in-tulum/))
**Who travels:** Domestic families, strongly. This is one of the best natural fits with the Mexican summer school holiday and one of the very few genuine demand drivers in your low season.
**Booking lead time:** 2 to 4 weeks.
**The offer: "Noches de Tortugas," July, August, September and October.**
A family package built around a **licensed, regulated turtle-release evening** through Flora, Fauna y Cultura de México or a comparable permitted programme, plus an in-house kids' conservation session and a Cidro early family dinner. Two hard rules: **book only through licensed programmes**, and **never promise a sighting.** Sell it as "the chance," not "the guarantee." A property that markets turtle tourism responsibly earns a real reputational asset in Tulum, where a lot of operators do not.

### Sargassum season: the honest one
**Dates: SEASONAL. Roughly April to October, peaking June to August.** May is the ramp-up, June and July are peak, August stays heavy, decline through September and October. **The clear-water window is November to March**, though conditions can change within 48 hours.
**Tulum is the worst-affected major destination on this coast.** Its south-facing open coast, the absence of protective reef structure, and limited heavy-equipment cleaning mean Tulum is typically the first major beach to receive sargassum and the last to clear. The University of South Florida Optical Oceanography Lab projected 2026 as likely the worst sargassum year on record, with early arrivals on Quintana Roo beaches in January and March, months ahead of the historical average.
Sources: [How Is The Sargassum, peak months guide](https://www.howisthesargassum.com/blog/sargassum-season-riviera-maya-peak-months), [How Is The Sargassum, Tulum live conditions](https://www.howisthesargassum.com/tulum), [Sargazo Watch, month-by-month](https://www.sargazowatch.com/blog/sargassum-season-riviera-maya).
**2027 is unknowable today.** Do not forecast it. Monitor USF's Sargassum Watch bulletins and the live trackers monthly from February.

**What an inland La Veleta property should actually do about it, and this is a structural advantage, not a spin exercise:**

**Position AMARI as the property that does not depend on the beach.**
1. **Stop putting beach photography at the top of your May-to-September marketing.** Lead with the pool, the cenotes, the villas and the courtyard. If a guest books a beach fantasy in July and gets a brown shoreline, you own a one-star review you did not have to earn.
2. **The offer: "Garantía Cenote."** From May to September, every stay of 3 nights or more includes a guided cenote visit. Cenotes are freshwater, inland, unaffected by sargassum, and there are dozens within twenty minutes of La Veleta. This converts your biggest seasonal liability into the reason to book you rather than a beachfront hotel: *"Our water is always clear. It is underground."*
3. **Publish honest sargassum status.** A short, dated, factual sargassum note on your website and in pre-arrival emails, linking a public tracker. Counter-intuitively this **increases** conversion, because the guests who book anyway have correctly set expectations and the ones who would have complained self-select out.
4. **Never promise a clear beach.** Not in an ad, not in a DM, not in a booking confirmation. This is the single most common reputational injury in Tulum hospitality.

# SECTION 4: LOW SEASON, AND WHAT AN OWNER SHOULD ACTUALLY DO

## 4.1 Which months are actually weak

**The weakest month is September.** Reported Tulum hotel occupancy fell from 66.7% to 49.2% year on year in one September, and September is repeatedly identified as the softest month. In an October report, Tulum beach-zone hotels were described at 15 to 20 percent occupancy with all-inclusive properties near 40 percent, attributed to sargassum, high attraction pricing and a general demand correction. ([Riviera Maya News, hoteliers seek credit restructuring](https://www.rivieramayanews.mx/tulum-hoteliers-seek-credit-restructuring-as-low-season-hits-occupancy/), [The Yucatan Times, Tulum occupancy](https://theyucatantimes.com/2025/10/whats-happening-in-tulum-empty-streets-and-hotels-with-barely-54-occupancy/), [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/travel/a-drop-in-tulum-hotel-occupancy/))

**The honest low-season map for AMARI:**

| Period | Severity | Why | Your counter-demand |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mid-Jan to early Feb** | Mild dip | Festival crowd leaves, Mexican cuesta de enero | Constitución puente (1 Feb), Arena Festival, long-stay nomads |
| **Late Apr to June** | Moderate | Sargassum ramps, no puente in 2027 (1 May is a Saturday), heat and humidity rise | **Día de las Madres (10 May, Monday)**, whale shark season opens, turtle nesting |
| **July to mid-Aug** | Mixed | Sargassum at or near peak, **but SEP summer holidays run from 9 July** | **Domestic family volume season.** Cenote-led, not beach-led |
| **Late Aug to Oct** | **Severe. This is the trough.** | Peak hurricane season, sargassum tail, no school holidays, foreign market absent | Independencia (15 to 16 Sep), autumn equinox (22 to 23 Sep), turtle hatching peak (Oct) |

**Note the shape: your low season is not a single block, it is May-June and late-August-to-October, with a domestic family peak in July and early August wedged in between.** Most Tulum owners treat May to October as one dead zone and miss the summer school window entirely. That window is confirmed by government calendar, not guesswork: the school year ends **9 July 2027** and the 2026 summer season drew **22.4 million tourists nationally with 65% average hotel occupancy**, a 6.5% increase on 2025. ([La Jornada / Sectur, summer 2026](https://www.jornada.com.mx/2026/07/19/economia/014n1eco))

## 4.2 What to actually do in the low season

Eight concrete moves, in order of return.

**1. Sell the property to a market that is not tourism: the 30-night residency.**
Your co-working space, gym, sauna, cold plunge and kitchen-equipped villas are a **long-stay product**, and long-stay demand is counter-cyclical to holiday demand. Launch **"Residencia AMARI," a 30-night rate at roughly 40 to 55% of the nightly rack rate**, available 1 May to 15 December excluding the Muertos window, including a dedicated co-working desk, weekly housekeeping, laundry, and a Cidro half-board option. Sell it to Mexican remote workers from CDMX and Monterrey (a peso-priced product with MSI) and separately to foreign digital nomads (a USD-priced product). One empty month at 45% of rack beats four scattered weekend nights at 100%.

**2. Turn the amenity stack into a local membership.**
Tulum and La Veleta have a permanent, growing resident population of expats, remote workers and business owners with **no good gym, no sauna, and no cold plunge**. Sell **"Club Cuarenta y Seis," a monthly local membership**: gym, yoga classes, sauna and cold plunge, pool access on weekdays, co-working day passes, and a standing discount at Cidro. Cap the membership at a number that protects guest experience (say 80 to 120). This is **recurring monthly revenue that does not care what occupancy is**, and the members become Cidro's off-season dinner covers. This is the highest-value idea in this section.

**3. Make Cidro a restaurant with its own business, not a hotel amenity.**
A hotel restaurant dies with the hotel. A neighbourhood restaurant does not. In low season, run Cidro on a **local-facing calendar**: a weekly *comida corrida* at a local price, a Sunday family lunch, a monthly chef's table, a Día de la Candelaria tamalada (2 February), the Grito dinner (15 September), the pib dinner (1 November). **Open the reservations to non-guests permanently.** In September, local covers may be the majority of your F&B revenue.

**4. Run the maintenance and capex calendar inside the trough, not randomly.**
Deep maintenance, repainting, pool resurfacing, landscaping, staff training and photography go in **late August to early October**, the one window where you cannot sell the rooms anyway. Shooting new photography in early October also puts fresh assets in hand before the December booking wave.

**5. Build the wedding and retreat business, which is booked in low season and consumed in high season.**
A 46-villa gated property with a restaurant, yoga studio and pool is **a wedding venue and a retreat venue** that happens to also sell rooms. Wedding and retreat leads convert in 6 to 14 months, so the low-season months are when you should be **selling 2028**, not discounting 2027. Build a **"Buyout de 46 Villas"** rate card: full-property exclusivity, 46 villas, Cidro catering, ceremony space, and pitch it to wedding planners in CDMX, Monterrey, Miami and Texas. One buyout replaces a month of scattered occupancy. Separately, sell the **yoga studio plus villas plus kitchen** as a turnkey retreat venue to yoga teachers and wellness coaches on a per-head revenue share, filling May, June and September with other people's marketing.

**6. Price for the short Mexican booking window, not the long foreign one.**
With the Mexican average at **28.48 days**, holding high rates until 10 days out and then panic-discounting loses you the domestic market entirely. In low season, run a **rolling 35-day domestic flash rate** in pesos, with MSI, released every Monday for the window 35 to 21 days out. Discipline beats improvisation.

**7. Do not run a flat discount. Run a value-add.**
A 40% rate cut trains the market and resets your ADR permanently. A 40% value-add (third night free, Cidro half-board included, cenote tour included, late checkout) protects the published rate and shifts the cost onto services with far better margins than the room.

**8. Accept that September is for other revenue, and plan it that way.**
September will not be filled by tourists. It can be filled by **Independencia locals (15 to 16 September), the autumn equinox wellness residency, long-stay residents, Club Cuarenta y Seis members, and one well-sold retreat.** Budget September on F&B, memberships and long stays. If you budget September on room nights you will be disappointed every year.
