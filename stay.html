La Veleta · Uptown Tulum

A whole villa, a private pool, and no sargassum in your holiday.

We are inland. From April to October the beach hotels are fighting seaweed and we are not.

Sargassum hits this coast April to October.

Tulum gets it worse than anywhere on the coast.

Nobody can promise you a clean beach. We will not.

So we put you inland, with a pool and the cenotes.
