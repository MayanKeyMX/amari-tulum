How it works

Four steps. Then you never think about food again.

  1. Send your targetsProtein, carbs, fats and calories. No numbers yet? Tell us your weight, training and goal and our team sets them with you.
  2. Choose your styleChef inspired, where our kitchen cooks a rotating menu of Yucatecan flavors built for performance. Or done your way: your proteins, your carbs, your sauces.
  3. We cook and weighEvery component goes on the scale. Every container is labelled with protein, carbs, fat and calories, so your tracking app takes ten seconds.
  4. Delivered, or ready at the barTo your villa, your cowork desk, or anywhere in Uptown, La Veleta, Aldea Zamá and Región 15. Or grab it at the Fuel Bar.

Two ways to eat

Chef inspired. Or done your way.

01

Chef inspired

Our kitchen writes the menu, you hit your numbers. Achiote, sour orange, charred chile and lime: the flavors of Yucatán rebuilt as high protein, clean carb plates.

  • Pollo pibil bowl with brown rice and sweet potato
  • Lean cochinita with black beans and pickled onion
  • Poc chuc steak with charred tomato salsa
  • Tikin xic fish with quinoa and greens
  • Chaya egg white scramble with tortillas

02

Done your way

You set the plate. Pick every component and the grams. We cook it clean, season it how you like it, and weigh it exactly.

  • Protein: chicken, steak, pork loin, fish, shrimp, eggs, tofu
  • Carbs: rice, quinoa, sweet potato, tortillas, oats
  • Veg: greens, grilled vegetables, chaya, cucumber
  • Sauces on the side: habanero, salsa verde, tahini, none
  • Allergies and no-go foods noted once, respected always
